WHAT IS THE SOUTH COLOURED BALL SERIES?

From beginners to the next Alex De Minaur, the South Coloured Ball Match Play Series caters to all our young players out there. It has been designed for Red Ball, Orange Ball and Green Ball players, allowing kids to compete at their own level and in a fun, friendly, inclusive and safe environment. Events are held around the Southern NSW region weekly, and players are allowed to decide where they would like to play and how often. Nothing is locked in!
