The Northumberland Interclub Series is back in 2025, offering team-based competition for players representing their clubs and home venues across the Central Coast, Newcastle & Hunter regions.

The 2025 series offers a cleaner, more consolidated playing season, with the following competitions available:

  • Open Division 1 (Premier)
  • Open Division 2
  • Junior (12/u, 14/u & 16/u)
  • Social

Entry to the Northumberland Interclub is open to all member clubs / venues in the Northumberland Region, provided minimum hosting criteria can be met.

The 2025 Interclub Guidelines provide complete information on the series, including how to register your club.

Contact your local club or coach to enter. Entries will close Thursday 31 July 2025.

Congratulations to our 2024 Winners:
  • Open Division 1: Total Tennis Lake Macquarie
  • Open Division 2: Bar Beach Tennis Club
  • 12 & under: Avoca Beach Tennis Club
  • 17 & under: Total Tennis Lake Macquarie
  • Social: Bolton Point Tennis Club
